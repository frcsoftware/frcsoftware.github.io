To execute these tasks, teams run automatic positioning programs rather than burdening the driver with such precise movements.
In Stage 1C, you will learn fundamental concepts pertaining to robot control algorithms.
You will also learn about how to log data from your robot beyond simple print statements.

## Key Concepts

Mechanisms have "states" such as position, angle, velocity, and acceleration.
For FRC purposes, a PID controller is an equation which takes a desired mechanism state, compares it to the current state, and outputs a voltage which your motor controllers are commanded to output.
By properly "tuning" a PID controller, you can make a mechanism quickly and precisely change state.
Telemetry is the process of recording and sending live data about your robot to a live output and/or log file.

## Stage 1C Goals

By the end of Stage 1C, you'll program a feature which enables your kitbot's drivetrain to automatically and smoothly turn in place to any given angle such that your kitbot can aim towards the hub on its own.
You will also be able to read data from your kitbot via logging with Telemetry.
